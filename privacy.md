# Privacy Policy

**Anew**

Last updated: 10/14/2026

## The short version

Anew keeps your affirmations, favorites, settings and reminders on your phone. There are no accounts, there is no server of ours, and nothing you write in the app is sent anywhere.

## What stays on your device

Everything you create or choose in Anew is stored in a database on your phone and nowhere else:

- the categories you select
- the affirmations you favorite
- the affirmations you write yourself
- your theme and font
- your reminder times and frequency
- how many consecutive days you have opened the app

We cannot see any of it. We have no copy of it. If you delete the app, it is gone, and we have no way to restore it.

## What we do not do

- We do not require an account, an email address, or a sign-in.
- We do not run analytics, tracking, advertising, or any third-party SDK that profiles you.
- We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect it.
- We do not track you across other apps or websites.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled by your phone, locally. The affirmation text is chosen on the device and never leaves it. If you turn notification permission off, the reminders simply stop.

## Subscriptions

Anew uses Apple's In-App Purchase system for its subscription, with RevenueCat as the service that checks whether a subscription is active.

- Apple processes the payment. We never see or receive your card details, billing address, or Apple Account.
- RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier generated for your installation, along with the purchase receipt Apple issues, so the app can tell whether your subscription is active. This identifier is not your name, email, or device ID, and it is not linked to anything you write in the app.
- Apple provides us with aggregate sales and subscription reports. These are totals and trends, not individual people.

RevenueCat's own privacy policy is at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple's is at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy.

## Diagnostics

If your phone is set to share crash and usage data with developers, Apple may send us crash reports. Those reports come from Apple, are aggregated, and contain no affirmation content. You can turn this off in your phone's settings under Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements.

## Children

Anew is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to delete, but if you have a concern please get in touch.

## Your rights

Because your data never leaves your phone, you are already in full control of it:

- To see your data, open the app.
- To change it, edit it in the app.
- To delete it, delete the app.

If you are in a region with laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, the practical effect is the same: we hold no personal data about you to disclose, correct, port, or erase.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
